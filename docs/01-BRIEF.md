# 01 赛事原文（AARON Awards 2027 · 08 Best Speculative Ad）

> 抓取时间 2026-11-19。原文见 https://www.aaronawards.com/ 与
> https://www.aaronawards.com/criteria-2027/index.html

## 类别定义（原文）

> **08 Best Speculative Ad**
> Celebrates speculative brand commercials that use AI to experiment, push creative boundaries
> and explore bold new territories beyond traditional production constraints. The work must take
> the form of a compelling, commercially relevant ad for a real brand, demonstrating how AI unlocks
> fresh creative territories, imaginative storytelling and new possibilities for brands.
>
> Entries must be created entirely using generative AI, with traditional post-production and finishing permitted.

## 评分（原文）

| 项 | 权重 |
| --- | --- |
| Creative Experimentation & Originality | 30% |
| Creative Idea & Storytelling | 25% |
| Craft & Quality of Execution | 20% |
| Innovative Use of AI | 15% |
| Brand Relevance & Commercial Credibility | 10% |

## 资格（原文，这一条是选它的唯一理由）

> ## General Eligibility
> **For all categories except Best Speculative Ad**, entries must meet all of the following:
> **01 Commercially Commissioned Work (Required)** — The project must have been commissioned by a
> real brand, client, organisation, or product owner.

也就是说：**21 个类别里，只有 Spec Ad 这一类不要求真实品牌委托**，
其余都硬性要求 commercially commissioned。这是独立创作者唯一能进的门。

其他两条通用规定仍然适用：

> **02 Entry Eligibility Period** — eligible work must have been publicly active or in production
> in the defined award period **15 February 2026 – 15 February 2027**.
>
> **03 Gen AI Must Play a Central Creative or Production Role** — narrative development /
> visual or sonic creation / real-time generation / workflow innovation / production efficiency /
> creative decision-making, human-machine collaboration.
